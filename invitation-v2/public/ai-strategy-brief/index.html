Company maturity groups. Remainder calculated: 100 − 7.5 − 41. Read the source ↗

The research points toward an operating challenge: adopting a tool is easier than changing how work gets done. Our companion analysis separates the evidence from the implications.

  1. Build governance into the work. Give an agent clear limits and an accountable owner.
  2. Track the business outcome. Connect the experiment to a KPI or a financial result.
  3. Redesign the workflow. Improve the process around the tool, then measure the result.
