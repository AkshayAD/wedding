Respect your table.

Rules version 22 October 2026 · Applies to the game, companion website and Fire TV app.

Adult players

Varsha House is for adults aged 18 or over. It contains a fictional murder investigation and forensic themes. Before creating, joining or resuming a room, confirm that you are 18 or older and accept these rules. Both choices require your explicit selection. You can decline and return to setup.

Your declaration is saved on this device with the rules version. It is self-declared confirmation; it does not verify your age or identity and is not an Amazon age-verification service. We do not request a date of birth or identity document. Review the rules or reset the confirmation in Settings > Table rules.

Fictional case only

Choose a made-up detective alias and keep shared notes about the fictional case. Do not use the game for real allegations or to investigate real people. Do not post harassment, hate, sexual exploitation or sexual content, threats, or private information about real people, including contact details, identification documents, credentials or sensitive personal information.

A shared room

Anyone with the room code or invitation can read unlocked evidence and notes, change progress, remove notes or report and hide notes and names for everyone. Detective selection is a reading role, not an authenticated identity or exclusive ownership. Share the code only with your group.

Remove for everyone deletes a note from the shared notebook. Report & hide asks for a reason and hides a note or replaces a detective name for the whole room when accepted by the service. Reporting a name does not ban a person or revoke their room access. These controls affect the whole group and require confirmation.

Content checks and review

Conservative service checks screen names and notes; they can miss unsuitable material or reject an innocent phrase. These checks do not promise complete moderation. Reports retain the original content, reason, identifiers and time privately for manual operator review, with up to 20 records per room. Player-facing responses do not include reported originals. Report records have no automatic erasure schedule; see the privacy notice for retention and deletion requests.

Contact

For help or a removal request, contact Akshay Dewalwar, publishing as Mystery Myths, at akshaydewalwar305@gmail.com. Include the room code when needed and describe the issue without unnecessarily repeating sensitive content. See Help & support for the in-game controls.
