Effective 22 October 2026 · This notice covers the Varsha House game, its companion website and its Fire TV app.

Varsha House is operated by Akshay Dewalwar under the publisher name Mystery Myths. Contact akshaydewalwar305@gmail.com about privacy, access or deletion requests.

What the game saves

When you create or use a room, the game service saves the detective aliases your group enters, the room code, chosen difficulty, shared notebook entries, pins, read confirmations, deduction completion status, number of accusation attempts, hints, pause state, progress and activity timestamps. These records make it possible to continue an investigation and share it across devices.

No game account or sign-in is required. Use made-up detective names and observations about the fictional case. Do not enter phone numbers, addresses, identification documents, passwords or other sensitive information into the notebook.

The browser or Fire TV app also stores room-resume information, detective selection, setup aliases, reading preferences, sound settings and a versioned adult and table-rules confirmation on that device. Clearing this local storage removes those saved preferences on that device; it does not delete the shared room.

Who can see a room

Anyone with a room code or invitation can read the unlocked evidence and shared notes and change the room's progress, remove notes and report and hide notes or detective names for the whole group. Room codes are access keys, rather than personal accounts. Share your invitation only with your own group.

The game has no public room directory or public notebook feed. Game records are available to the operator and infrastructure providers as needed to run, maintain and support the service. If you use your device's Share option, your chosen sharing app receives the link you choose to share.

Shared notes and reports

Names and notebook entries are screened with conservative server-side rules. These checks can miss unsuitable content or reject an innocent phrase. They are not a guarantee of safety. Each note offers Remove for everyone and Report & hide. The detective chooser offers Report & hide name. Reports hide the note or replace the alias in the shared room immediately after the service accepts the action.

Reports include the reason, room and item identifiers, time and the reported original text or alias. The service keeps up to 20 private report records per room for manual operator review; these originals are not included in player-facing room responses. Reporting a name does not authenticate, ban or remove a person, or revoke the room code. Report records may remain with room records until operational cleanup or an accepted deletion request; there is no automatic report-erasure schedule.

Hosting and technical information

The website uses GitHub Pages. The game service uses OpenAI Sites infrastructure, including Cloudflare database and file services. Requests to those providers necessarily transmit technical information such as IP address, browser or app details, request time and requested URLs. A room invitation or requested URL can contain your room code. Infrastructure providers may process and retain operational, security and diagnostic logs under their own policies.

The current developer-controlled game does not integrate an advertising SDK, advertising identifier, purchases or behavioral advertising. A version distributed through Amazon may also involve Amazon's Appstore services and their operational or analytics processing. This notice does not promise that infrastructure or distribution providers keep no logs.

Recordings and music are streamed to you. The game does not access your microphone or record your voice. Browser voice fallback uses your device or browser's speech service when you explicitly choose it. QR scanning takes place in the camera app you choose on your phone; the Fire TV app itself does not request camera access.

How long records remain

A room becomes inaccessible after 30 days without a saved game action. Simply reading a room does not extend that period. Expiry is an access limit; it is not a promise of automatic erasure at that exact time. Stored room records and provider backups or logs can remain until removed through operational cleanup or an accepted deletion request.

To request removal of a room, email the support address with the room code and your request. Do not attach identity documents. We may ask for relevant context to avoid removing someone else's investigation. Deleting a shared room affects the whole group; provider logs or backups may follow separate retention schedules. Leaving a room or uninstalling the app does not itself request server deletion.

Audience and choices

This fictional murder investigation is for adults aged 18 or over. Before creating, joining or resuming a room, each device asks for an explicit adult declaration and acceptance of the table rules. Neither choice is preselected. This is self-declared confirmation, not verified identity or age assurance. The game does not collect a date of birth or identity document for this purpose. The written records and narration contain murder and forensic themes. You may play without entering real names, mute music and narration, keep the invitation private, clear local storage, reset the device confirmation in Settings > Table rules, decline and return to setup, or stop using the game.
